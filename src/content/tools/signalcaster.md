SignalCaster automates the repetitive part of content distribution: you publish an article, then manually copy the title, description, image, and link into four different platforms. Connect an RSS or Atom feed and it detects new items, prepares the post, and publishes to your connected destinations on a schedule you set, so the work happens once rather than once per platform.

The organising idea is publishing groups. Feeds, destinations, schedules, and brands are bundled into reusable sets, which matters if you're running several sites or managing more than one client. You choose when content is checked, prepared, and published, then review statuses, logs, and diagnostics in one place rather than guessing whether something went out.

It's mobile-first, running as an Android and iOS app rather than a web dashboard, which suits solo operators and small teams more than large marketing departments. The free plan covers limited sources and publishing so you can test the workflow properly, with Creator at $9.99/month for more sources and platforms, and Business at $19.99/month for agency-scale use. It's honest that it isn't a design suite, an ads manager, or a substitute for an actual social strategy.
