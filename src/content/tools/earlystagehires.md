earlystagehires is a curated job board for people who want to join a startup early, founding roles, fractional execs, and first-10-hire positions at companies before they're household names. Roles are organized by function (engineering, product, design, growth, sales, ops) and filterable by country, with everything checked rather than scraped wholesale, so the list stays small and genuinely early-stage instead of drowning in enterprise and recruiter noise.

A talent-signals section adds something most boards don't: a running tracker of recent tech layoffs, pulled from public reports, framed around what it means for early-stage hiring. When a big company sheds thousands of senior engineers, that's a hiring window for a seed-stage team that normally can't get near that talent, and the board surfaces exactly that.

Browsing is free and the board updates daily. Companies can post roles through the site's Post a Job flow. It's built for a specific person, someone who wants ownership and upside over big-company comfort, rather than as a general-purpose job search.
