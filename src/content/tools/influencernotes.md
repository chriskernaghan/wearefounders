Influencer Notes is a writing workspace built specifically for planning YouTube videos, rather than a generic doc or an AI that spits out a finished script. It structures every video around the parts that actually drive retention, hook, promise, tension, payoff, and CTA, and keeps camera directions, on-screen text, research, and filming cues alongside the words so the whole plan lives in one place.

Two things set it apart from a plain editor. You can compare different hook variants side by side without losing earlier versions, useful when the opening is the part that makes or breaks a video, and a built-in timer estimates spoken runtime as you write, calculated locally in the browser so the script isn't uploaded anywhere. There's AI-assisted feedback on clarity and retention risks too, but it flags rather than rewrites, leaving the creator's voice intact.

The public script-template library and the runtime timer can both be used without an account. Beyond the free plan, paid access is $3.50/month or $35.88/year, with no free-trial countdown. It's aimed at YouTube creators and founder-led teams using video to grow an audience, not at anyone wanting one-click faceless-video generation or enterprise approval chains.
