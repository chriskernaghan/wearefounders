SoftSync is an SMB CRM built around the idea that you shouldn't have to open it. It connects to Claude and ChatGPT, so updating a record, checking a lead, or pulling a report happens in whatever conversation you're already having rather than in a separate tab you forget to open.

Underneath that it's a fairly complete workspace: leads, email and SMS outreach, booking pages, forms, documents with e-signature, and customer support all writing to a single customer record. The meeting assistant is the piece worth calling out, because it runs as a desktop app and captures notes locally rather than sending a bot to sit in your call, which avoids the awkwardness of a third participant appearing on a sales call.

Pricing is $29 per seat a month on Pro billed yearly, or $39 monthly, with Business at $59 and $79 respectively. Pro caps a workspace at five people and Business at 25, so team size pushes the tier rather than feature needs. The trial runs 14 days without a card, and teams migrating from another CRM are offered six months of Business credit per seat.

Worth knowing that the security posture is described as SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 "ready", meaning aligned rather than audited, which matters if you're selling into anyone who asks for the certificate. The customer logos and testimonials are real but cluster heavily around the Nordic startup scene, so there's less to go on if you're outside it.
