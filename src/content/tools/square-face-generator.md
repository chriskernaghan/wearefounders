Square Face Generator is a small, deliberately nostalgic avatar maker, the kind of part-picker that powered the classic Flash icon creators. You work through tabs for skin, bangs, side hair, eyes, nose, mouth, eyebrows, cheeks, glasses, and hats, assembling a square-faced character by hand, then hit save to download a 256x256 PNG.

There's no AI and no generation happening here: every choice is manual, which is the charm of it. What you pick is what you get, and the result is a distinctive little icon that works nicely as a profile picture or placeholder character.

It runs entirely in the browser as a preserved version of the original tool, with no signup and no tracking. Desktop with a mouse is the smoother experience given the classic interface. It's a fun, low-stakes tool rather than a professional design suite, and that's exactly the point.
