GraphMake takes the opposite approach to most design tools. Instead of dropping you onto a blank canvas with a hundred options, it splits into 93 single-purpose makers, one for bar charts, one for org charts, one for timelines, one for invoices, each doing a single job. You click directly on the artwork, type your numbers or labels, and download a PNG, usually inside a minute. There's a live chart on the homepage you can edit without signing up, which tells you more in ten seconds than any feature list would.

The makers sit in six groups: charts and graphs, diagrams and flows, infographics and content, documents and printables, shapes and backgrounds, and utilities like colour palettes and a contrast checker. The documents side is more useful than it first sounds, invoices, quotations and receipts keep sequential numbering, saved clients, and your brand details once you sign in, so it doubles as a light invoicing tool rather than just a graphic maker.

The free tier is genuinely free: no account needed for the core makers, and PNG exports come out without a watermark, which is unusual in this category. Pro is $19/year and adds SVG export for Figma or Illustrator, HD PNG up to 4x for print, animated MP4 charts, and unlimited saved documents. It's honest about its ceiling too, this is for turning information into a clear visual quickly, not for pixel-perfect brand work, GIS analysis, or desktop publishing.
