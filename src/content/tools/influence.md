Influence is a social scheduler organised around the week rather than the individual post. You plan a campaign as a set, so an announcement, a customer story, and a reminder sit together on one calendar with their publishing dates, and you review the whole run before any of it goes out. For a founder doing social in one sitting on a Monday, that framing fits better than a queue you top up post by post.

Influence IQ handles the drafting, working from a brief and your brand guidelines rather than an empty box. What makes that useful is the per-account preview: each destination shows its own version of the wording and the selected photos or videos, so you catch the LinkedIn post that reads like a tweet before it publishes rather than after. Recurring series let you hang content off an ongoing theme instead of rebuilding the structure each week.

It connects to more than 20 platforms, and there's an agent layer so ChatGPT or Claude can schedule into it directly. Beyond scheduling there's a reply inbox, link and QR customisation, and post analytics.

Pricing starts at $19 a month or $190 a year for Starter, with Growth and Pro above it. There's no ongoing free plan and the seven-day trial takes billing details, converting unless you cancel, so go in knowing that. Nothing publishes without you reviewing it, which rules it out if you wanted something fully hands-off.
