PayoutPeek answers a question every marketplace seller has asked at some point, which is where the money actually went. You download your monthly statement from Etsy, drop it in, and it separates the deposit into its component parts: listing fees, transaction fees, payment processing, ads, refunds, and sales tax, ending at what you genuinely kept. TikTok Shop gets the same treatment, and the paid tier puts both side by side.

The privacy approach is the part that makes it easy to recommend. The file is read and analyzed inside your browser tab rather than being uploaded, and it clears when you close the tab. Product costs you enter stay in that browser too. For anyone reluctant to hand their full sales history to a third party, that removes the objection entirely.

Free covers one monthly statement at a time with the real take-home figure, a full fee breakdown, and the top red flags. Pro at $8 a month or $69 a year adds per-product profit using your own costs, order-level detail, up to 12 months combined, and CSV export. Multi at $14 a month brings Etsy and TikTok Shop together.

Two honest limits. It reads an exported CSV rather than connecting to your Etsy account, so you're fetching a file each month, and it covers those two marketplaces only. Worth noting the sample shops on the site are clearly labeled as illustrative rather than dressed up as customers, which is a small thing that says something about how it's being run.
