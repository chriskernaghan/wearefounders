Foxit PDF Editor lets you edit text and images in PDFs, convert files to and from Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, and create forms without relying on Adobe Acrobat. It also handles scanned documents with OCR, so you can turn a paper contract or invoice into a searchable file.

For founders dealing with proposals, agreements, and client paperwork, the draw is having editing, signatures, security, and document management in one place. It works on Windows, macOS, and the web, with mobile apps included in PDF Editor+. Team collaboration tools and a document management system help when more than one person needs to review a file.

Subscriptions start at $10.99 per user per month, with annual plans and a one-time desktop licence also available. There is a 14-day free trial, but the full editor is not free. Some useful extras, including mobile editing, AI-powered redaction, and larger AI allowances, sit behind more expensive plans or add-ons.
