LocalVeil Studio is a Windows desktop app for redacting sensitive information out of screenshots, PDFs, Word documents, and Excel workbooks, entirely on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded: OCR, detection, masking, and export all run inside the installed app, with no account, API key, analytics, or cloud storage involved. Originals are never touched; it rebuilds clean copies as fresh files with the metadata stripped.

The toolkit covers image masking (solid, pixelate, or blur), page-by-page PDF redaction that flattens out the original text layer, and redacted DOCX and XLSX copies that also catch comments, headers, and hidden worksheets. A free watermarked Preview is the actual app rather than a mockup, so you can open real files and see the workflow before paying.

It's unusually candid about its own boundaries, which is a good sign in a privacy tool. It tells you solid masks are safer than blur for real concealment, that automatic detection still needs a human review pass, and that it's a practical aid rather than a compliance guarantee. It publishes a SHA-256 release hash and scan results so you can verify the download, and pricing is a one-time $29 for an individual, with Team ($249) and Agency ($499) licenses for internal multi-seat use.
