Autodisc is hosting built around one specific need: getting Discord bots online fast and keeping them responsive. Deploys ship in seconds with live logs, and region-aware routing plus real-time health checks keep latency-sensitive bots running without the manual babysitting that comes with general-purpose hosting.

Everything sits in one control plane rather than being scattered across separate dashboards: deploys, log search across gateway events and crashes, secrets management for tokens and API keys, and instant rollbacks to a previous release if something breaks. Automatic failover is built in, so a bad deploy doesn't mean extended downtime.

Pricing stays simple rather than tiered into confusing add-ons: Builder is $3/month, or $2.50/month billed yearly, for one small full-stack project, and Starter is $5/month for side projects and early bots. It's aimed at developers, indie teams, and Discord bot builders who want managed deployment without setting up their own infrastructure, not at teams needing bare-metal servers or bespoke enterprise compliance.
