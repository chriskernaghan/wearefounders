PushOwl combines email marketing, SMS, and browser push notifications for Shopify stores. Rather than using separate tools for each channel, you can create email campaigns, collect subscribers with popups, and send messages tied to customer activity. Its Shopify focus makes it a practical option for store owners who want to keep customer communication close to their shop data.

The useful part is the automation. Back-in-stock and price-drop alerts give shoppers a reason to return, while abandoned-cart reminders can help recover orders that might otherwise be lost. There are customizable email templates, audience segments, and campaign reports to help you test what gets a response. SMS can also handle order and shipping updates. The precise features depend on your plan, with more advanced web push workflows reserved for paid tiers.

You can start on the free plan, which includes 500 emails and 500 web push notifications per month, with no cap on your number of contacts. Paid web push bundles begin at $19 per month, and email volume and SMS credits carry their own usage costs. A 14-day trial is available. That makes PushOwl worth considering if you run a Shopify store and want several retention channels under one roof, but it is not designed for non-Shopify businesses. If you send frequent campaigns, check the cost of each channel before committing.
