Lumi is a voice-first executive assistant built for owner-run companies that run on group chats instead of project software. A voice note between jobs, a pasted thread, a photo of a quote, or a spreadsheet gets turned into tracked promises, flagged risks, and open money questions, then rolled into one evidence-linked morning brief showing what needs a decision, what is overdue, and who is blocked.

Every fact traces back to the words it came from, so nothing enters the record without a source. Lumi asks before acting: money, deletions, and messages to outsiders always require confirmation first. The team is explicit that this is not an autonomous agent that sends things on its own.

Not a fit for teams wanting a generic task list, a fully autonomous agent, or a mature enterprise suite that already covers this workflow. Best suited to owner-run businesses of roughly 5 to 100 people, contractors, distributors, clinics, agencies, trading houses, and service firms that live in group chats rather than dedicated software.
