Lumi is a voice-first executive assistant built for owner-run companies that run on group chats instead of project software. A voice note between jobs, a pasted thread, a photo of a quote, or a spreadsheet gets turned into tracked promises, flagged risks, and open money questions, then rolled into one evidence-linked morning brief showing what needs a decision, what is overdue, and who is blocked.

Every fact traces back to the words it came from, so nothing enters the record without a source. That receipts-first approach is the core trust mechanic: if Lumi can't point to where something came from, it doesn't get written down.

## Who it's for

Owner-run businesses of roughly 5 to 100 people, contractors, distributors, clinics, agencies, trading houses, and service firms that already live in group chats rather than dedicated software. It's built Vietnamese-first with 62 languages total, so it fits teams running on WhatsApp or Zalo threads just as well as email.

## What sets it apart

Lumi asks before acting. Money, deletions, and messages to outsiders always require confirmation first, and anything it does on its own undoes within a set window. That's a deliberate stance against the fully autonomous agent trend, and it's the right one for teams who want the record kept, not the decisions made for them.

Not a fit if you're after a generic task list, a fully autonomous agent that sends messages or moves money without approval, or a mature enterprise suite that already handles this workflow well.
