TableSpark is a website builder made specifically for independent UK restaurants rather than a generic small-business tool. Photograph a printed menu and its AI reads every dish, price, dietary mark, and spice level, then drafts a full site you review line by line before anything publishes. Pick from 50 restaurant-ready templates, edit inline like a slide deck, and you can be live on a free tablespark.uk address in an afternoon.

The core pitch is keeping the restaurant's guests, data, and money its own. Bookings and online orders run at 0% commission, so there's no cut taken on a reservation, and a single menu acts as the source of truth: change a price once and it updates across the hero, menu page, specials, and structured data everywhere at once.

It's refreshingly upfront about what isn't ready. Custom domains with managed SSL are marked as still being activated rather than presented as live, the analytics figures on the marketing site are clearly labelled as illustrative, and it notes that Stripe's standard card fees still apply on top of the zero commission. Building is free until you publish, then plans run £19/month (Starter), £39 (Growth, with bookings), and £69 (Full, up to five sites).
