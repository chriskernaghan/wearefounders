MeDo is an AI app builder aimed at founders who want to get from idea to working prototype without a development setup. You describe what you want in plain language, and it generates the interface, structures the user flow, and creates a connected database when the app needs one, then publishes the result to a hosted URL you can share.

Beyond web apps, it targets mobile too, with a workflow for building and publishing mobile applications, including a route to Google Play. Worth setting expectations here: any store publishing still passes through the platform's own review and signing steps, so "one-click" smooths the process rather than removing it entirely. A library of ready-made skills extends what you can build, covering things like content generation, data handling, and basic automation.

Pricing is on the accessible end for this category: a free tier with daily credits to experiment, and a Pro plan at $18/month with a larger daily allowance. It's best understood as a fast way to build lightweight apps, landing pages, internal tools, and MVPs, rather than a replacement for a full engineering build on anything complex.
